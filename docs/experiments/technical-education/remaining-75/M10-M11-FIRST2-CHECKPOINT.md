# Positions 36-40: M10 and the first two M11 lessons

Authored scope: M10-L01, M10-L02, M10-L03, M11-L01 and M11-L02 only. M11-L03 and later lessons were deliberately not started. This scoped checkpoint is additive to baseline `9fd1e9c7`; the coordinator owns aggregate counts and publishing.

## Deliverables

- 20 independent localized packages: Egyptian Arabic, Modern Standard Arabic, Gulf Arabic and English. Each contains three explanatory sections, a worked case and decision, an explained quiz, a practical assignment/rubric and a contextual FAQ.
- 15 distinct concept diagrams and 60 localized SVG exports, using the existing renderer and palette.
- 40 PDFs: 20 three-page workbooks and 20 one-page worksheets; 80 pages rendered for review.
- 20 contextual, six-scene video-text files. Display arithmetic uses directional isolates and nonbreaking spaces; spoken strings contain neither isolates nor nonbreaking spaces.

All 164 pre-existing PDFs retain their SHA-256 hashes. The diagram and PDF-revision maps preserve every baseline entry and add only this scope's 15 and 40 entries respectively. No UI, rendering pipeline, shared production manifest, media mapping or Site deployment was changed by this workstream.

## Teaching and source review

The selected readable source pages and source identity are recorded in `m10-m11-first2-evidence/source-anchors.json`. They informed original conceptual teaching and were not copied into learner assets. In particular, source screen-height and viewing-distance charts are not treated as universal requirements. Dining clearances, 300 mm table projections and screen coordinates are explicitly declared calculation exercises, not accessibility, structural or installation approvals.

The common facts remain aligned across all four versions: the dining envelope is 2900 mm under its stated assumptions; the centered table-base example projects 300 mm per end; the group has three types and six physical pieces; the 700 mm screen envelope centered at 1100 mm has bottom/top coordinates of 750/1450 mm. Floor bearing, anti-tip restraint, cabinet suspension and screen mounting are distinguished. Substrate, fasteners and performance require actual system information and qualified review.
